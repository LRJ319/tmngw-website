02 · Character

The Collector

The face of the brand has no face. A hooded figure in a long dark coat, head lost in shadow, hands raised — and the bottles rise with them. He doesn't pick up fragrance. He calls it.

Character rules

  • Never show a face. The hood stays up. Shadow does the work.
  • Hands are the hero. Black gloves + one gold ring. Same on the avatar and in real footage so the two match.
  • Bottles orbit, never sit. On thumbnails and intros they float — magnetic, controlled, calm.
  • Gold = control, violet = energy. Gold glow on the hands, violet field lines between hand and bottle.
  • He's unbothered. Posture is relaxed. Power without trying.

Where he shows up

Profile pictureIntro stingThumbnailsOutro cardMerch laterStory highlights

Real footage = your gloved hands + bottles. Avatar = brand moments. Your voice ties both together.

Pushback: faceless only works if something else is instantly recognizable. Lock three signatures and never break them — the gloves + ring, your voice, and the 2-second bottle-rise open. If a viewer can't tell it's Slick Scents with the sound off in the first second, the frame isn't on-brand.

05 · Signature Open

The 2-second bottle rise

Every video opens the same way so the feed learns it. Keep it under 2 seconds on Shorts/Reels/TikTok — the hook lands at 0:02. YouTube long-form can run the full 5-second version.

0:00.0

Black frame

Bottle sits in darkness. Low bass hum starts.

0:00.4

Gloves enter

Gloved hands rise into frame, palms open. Gold glow under them.

0:00.9

Bottle lifts

Bottle floats up (string/clear-stand or edit). Violet field lines. "Whoosh."

0:01.4

Logo snap

Hard cut to logo for 6 frames. Snap / spray sound.

0:02.0

"Let's get slick."

VO line + hook text on screen. Content starts.

Outro (last 3 sec)

  • Visual: hands lower, bottle drops back to the table, avatar end card
  • VO: "Cop, wait, or pass — tell me in the comments. Stay slick."
  • Text: next video teaser or "Part 2 tomorrow"

Sound kit

  • Hum — low sub-bass bed under the open
  • Whoosh — on bottle lift
  • Spray / snap — on logo hit
  • Music bed — lo-fi or smooth R&B instrumental, ducked under VO

Film once in 9:16. Cut the same footage three ways. Don't make separate content for each app — change the hook, caption, and length.

10 · Production

Make the avatar for real

The SVG on this page is the concept. Use this prompt in Adobe Firefly, Midjourney, or Canva to generate the polished version. Generate 10, pick one, then reuse that image as the style reference so every version stays consistent.

Cinematic portrait of a faceless hooded figure in a long matte-black tailored coat, face completely hidden in deep shadow under the hood, both gloved hands raised at shoulder height with palms open, a thin gold ring on one finger. Five luxury cologne bottles float in a perfect arc above the hands, suspended by glowing violet magnetic field lines. Warm gold light glows from the palms. Dark obsidian background, subtle smoke, gold and violet rim lighting, high contrast, premium fragrance-ad aesthetic, 4:5 composition, ultra detailed, photoreal.

Avoid: visible face, eyes, helmets, capes, superhero costumes, logos or brand names on bottles, text.

Gear (starter)

  • Phone on tripod
  • 1 key light + 1 RGB rim light
  • Lav or USB mic for VO
  • Black gloves, gold ring
  • Clear acrylic stand (fake the float)

Edit stack

  • CapCut — save the open/outro as a template
  • Auto-captions in Inter Bold, gold highlight on key word
  • Bottle mark watermark, bottom right
  • Export 1080×1920

Track (monthly)

  • Avg watch % by template
  • Which hook version won (A vs B)
  • Saves on IG, shares on TikTok
  • Top 3 comment requests → next month's reviews
