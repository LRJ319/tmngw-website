# The Stock Check — Protocol for New Investors

Five steps to grade a stock and size a position to the money you actually have.
Interactive version: `invest.html`. Educational only — not personalized financial advice.

---

## Step 1 — Ready Check (decides your investable capital)

Before picking any stock, your cash goes in this order:

1. **Emergency fund** — 3–6 months of expenses in high-yield savings.
2. **High-interest debt** (~8%+ APR) — paid off. Killing 24% card debt is a guaranteed 24% return.
3. **Employer match** — captured.
4. **5-year rule** — only invest money you won't need for 5+ years.

```
Investable capital = Cash − (Emergency target − Emergency saved) − High-interest debt
```

## Step 2 — Red Flags (any one = Pass)

- Share price under $5
- Market cap under $2B
- Negative free cash flow 3 years running
- Share count up 10%+ in a year (dilution)
- Accounting restatement, SEC probe, or auditor change
- Found it via TikTok / group chat / "about to run" hype
- Can't explain how it makes money in one sentence
- Up 100%+ in the last 3 months

## Step 3 — Scorecard (10 checks × 0–2 pts = 20)

| Group | Check | 2 pts | 1 pt | 0 pts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Business | Revenue growth (3-yr avg) | 10%+/yr | 0–10% | Shrinking |
| Business | Profitable (net income + FCF) | Both positive | One | Neither |
| Business | Operating margin | 15%+ | 5–15% | <5% |
| Business | Competitive edge | Clear & durable | Some | Easy to copy |
| Health | Debt-to-equity | <0.5 | 0.5–1.5 | >1.5 |
| Health | Current ratio | >1.5 | 1.0–1.5 | <1.0 |
| Health | Return on equity | 15%+ | 8–15% | <8% |
| Price | P/E vs. 5-yr avg & peers | Below | In line | Well above / none |
| Price | PEG ratio | <1.0 | 1.0–2.0 | >2.0 or negative |
| Price | Insider signal | Buying | Neutral | Heavy selling |

Sources: Yahoo Finance (Statistics, Financials, Analysis tabs), your brokerage app, or the 10-K.

## Step 4 — Verdict & Position Size

| Score | Verdict | Position |
|---|---|---|
| 16–20 | **Strong Buy** | 100% of max position |
| 12–15 | **Starter Buy** | 50% of max position |
| 8–11 | **Watchlist** | $0 — re-check after earnings |
| 0–7 or any red flag | **Pass** | $0 |

| Risk comfort | Individual-stock bucket | Max per stock |
|---|---|---|
| Conservative | 10% of investable | 3% of investable |
| Moderate | 15% | 5% |
| Aggressive | 20% | 7% |

The rest (80–90%) goes to a low-cost index-fund core (total market or S&P 500).

**Example:** $5,000 investable, moderate, score 16 → max per stock $250 → Strong Buy → $250 in 3 monthly buys of ~$83.

## Step 5 — Buy Plan & Hold Rules

- **Strong Buy:** 3 equal buys, one per month.
- **Starter Buy:** half now, half in 3 months. Add the rest only if it re-scores 16+.
- Write a one-sentence thesis. Re-score every quarter after earnings.
- Down 20%: re-score. Still 12+ with the thesis intact — hold or add. Otherwise sell.
- Trim any single stock that grows past 10% of the portfolio.
