What We Are

The Show

This Might Not Go Well is a culture podcast for the intellectual who loves to talk that slick talk. Three voices from New York delivering unfiltered takes on hip-hop, politics, sports, and the social debates that define a generation.

Launched in 2020, the show has published over 637 episodes and built a loyal community of weekly listeners who return for honest conversation that social media timelines won't allow.

Listed in Feedspot's top Black Podcast directory and distributed on 8+ platforms, TMNGW has grown from a New York City conversation into a nationally recognized voice in independent Black media.

The Voices

Meet the Hosts

Three different perspectives. One shared mission: real talk, no filter.

B Brezzy
@Brezzy_Papi Follow
Co-Host & Creator

B Brezzy is the energy that sets the tone for every episode. Born and bred in New York City, he brings a raw authenticity and a perspective shaped by life on the ground in one of the world's most culturally rich cities.

His takes are sharp, his delivery is unmatched, and his ability to pivot from a hip-hop deep dive to a political conversation in seconds is what keeps listeners locked in from the jump.

Slick Grayson
@King__Law Follow
Co-Host & Analyst

Slick Grayson is the analytical backbone of the show. Methodical, measured, and always ready with a rebuttal, he brings an intellectual depth that elevates every topic from surface-level take to genuine discourse.

Whether it's breaking down a sports stat, dissecting a political move, or challenging the group with a counterargument nobody saw coming — Slick keeps the conversation honest.

Jamie Toxx
@Respectfully_AG Follow
Co-Host & Culture Correspondent

Jamie Toxx is the culture lens. With a sharp eye for what's happening in music, sports, and the broader social conversation, Jamie brings a perspective that connects the dots between entertainment and real life.

Charismatic, well-read, and never afraid to go there — Jamie Toxx is the voice that makes the show feel both grounded and ahead of the curve at the same time.
