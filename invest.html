Hold Rules
  • Write your reason for buying in one sentence. That's your thesis.
  • Re-run this check every quarter after earnings.
  • Down 20%? Re-score it. Thesis intact and score still 12+ — hold or add the next tranche. Score dropped or a red flag showed up — sell.
  • Never let one stock grow past 10% of your portfolio. Trim it back.
  • Don't check the price daily. Check the business quarterly.

Educational tool from the TMNGW team, not personalized financial advice. Numbers are guidelines — do your own research or talk to a fiduciary advisor before investing.
